TARABA STATE INVESTMENT PROMOTION AGENCY

Overview:

The Taraba State Investment Promotion Agency (TSIPA) was created in 2024 to coordinate investment promotion, improve the business environment and make Taraba an attractive destination for investment.
TSIPA is a One-Stop Investment Centre for business and investments in Nigeria. It drives the government’s investment objectives and is responsible for overseeing all investment activities in the State.
As part of our duties, we facilitate investments in the State by coordinating all the activities of Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) in the business environment. Our approach ensures the realization of the maximum benefits of policies and regulations in the State. We have a reputation for providing modernized, efficient, and transparent services to our clients with a view to reducing the cost of doing business in the State.
m Objectives: The primary objectives of TSIPA comprise the following: Promoting Taraba State as an Investment Destination: Showcasing the State’s investment opportunities, natural resources, and competitive advantages to attract investors.
Facilitating Investments: Providing support services, such as business registration, licensing, and permits, to ease the investment process.
Encouraging Public-Private Partnerships: Collaborating with private sector investors to develop and implement projects in key sectors, such as infrastructure, agriculture and manufacturing. Developing and Implementing Investment Policies: Formulating and implementing policies to create a favourable business environment and attract investments.
